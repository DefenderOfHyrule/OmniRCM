# OmniRCM Web injector

A WebUSB based payload injector for the Nintendo Switch.

## Requirements

(Requirements are also covered on the web injector page itself.)

- A device running ChromeOS, Linux, macOS or Android
- A chromium based browser (Chromium, Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, etc.)
- A good quality USB A to C cable (recommended) or USB C to C cable if your device lacks USB A ports

## How to use

Usage is simple:

1. Visit the [OmniRCM Web injector](https://omnircm.nintendohomebrew.com/web/),
1. [Put your switch in RCM](https://switch.hacks.guide/user_guide/rcm/entering_rcm),
1. Plug your Switch into your device via USB,
1. Click `Connect device...` and select the `APX` device,
1. Download a payload from the `Download Payloads` section,
1. Click `Add payload...` and select the newly downloaded payload (`.bin` file),
1. Click `Inject` and observe the injection result.

## Troubleshooting

For extra troubleshooting, you can also enable the Log at the top of the page. The Log contains slightly more information than the injection result, to provide assistance with figuring out the cause of an issue if present.
